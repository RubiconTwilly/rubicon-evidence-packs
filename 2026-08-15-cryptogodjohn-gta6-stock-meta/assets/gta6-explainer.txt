WHAT GTA6 IS AND WHY IT IS IN THIS VIDEO

GTA6 is an independent Solana meme token. It is not issued, sponsored or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

John's idea combines three things: worldwide attention around Grand Theft Auto VI, Rockstar's August 27 Extended Look, and a Raydium market where the meme token trades against Backpack Securities' tokenized TTWO asset.

StonkFun says eligible GTA6 holders receive part of the pool's trading fees in tokenized TTWO. That is a platform reward mechanic, not a Rockstar dividend and not proof that the GTA6 token is backed by Take-Two shares.
